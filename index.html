Real-estate-denominated markets

Every coin here is tethered to a piece of property.

Parcel is a launchpad on Robinhood Chain where every market is paired with a property-class coin instead of a stablecoin. Pick a class — a tin storage shed, a friend's couch, an RV, a villa — and the token you launch trades against that class's index price from the first buy.
