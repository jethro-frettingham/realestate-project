How it works

Parcel is a launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Every market is paired with a property-class coin instead of a stablecoin — a shed, a couch, a villa, a high-rise unit. The mechanics below are identical no matter which class a launch picks.

Property-class coins

Each of the 20 classes on the class list has a coin that tracks an index price for that class. A market's USD price is always:

token USD priceprice in pair coin × pair coin's USD index

Real estate doesn't trade on a continuous exchange the way gold or wheat does, so each class's index is a rolling median of recent comparable sale and listing data for that class, submitted by a set of oracle reporters and aggregated on-chain by PriceOracle.sol. A single-sided liquidity pool keeps each class coin buyable and sellable against USDG at that index price, the same role a Uniswap pool plays for a commodity coin. For classes with no reliable data feed yet — a friend's couch, a lean-to — the index is a fixed, published reference price maintained by the Parcel team until a real data source exists; that's disclosed on the class's own page.

Launch terms

Every launch follows the same curve, whatever it's paired with.

Mint and open

A launch mints 1,000,000,000 tokens. 800 million sell on a virtual-liquidity curve that opens at a $5,000 market cap and completes at $35,000, both fixed in the pair coin at creation. 200 million are reserved for migration.

Creator sets the fee and buys first

The creator picks a trading fee between 1% and 3% and makes a first buy of at least $1 in the same transaction that creates the market — a market can't exist with zero buyers. Buys can be paid in ETH, USDG, or the pair coin directly; ETH and USDG are swapped to the pair coin on the way in.

Trade on the curve

Anyone can buy or sell against the curve while it's open. Price moves along a constant-product virtual curve — no external liquidity is at risk during this phase, since the reserves are virtual until migration.

Migrate

When the last curve token sells, the pair coin raised and the reserved 200 million tokens move into a Uniswap v4 pool in the same transaction, at the curve's final price. The pool charges the same fee the creator chose, enforced by the pool itself, and its liquidity is permanent — it isn't withdrawable by the creator or by Parcel.

Fees

Every fee is split the same way on the curve and in the pool after migration:

Market metadata

A launch stores its image, name, and links in content-addressed storage. The permanent metadata URI is written on chain at creation and never changes, so a market's name, image, and links stay exactly what its creator published — including for the joke listings.

What this repo doesn't do yet

This is a working front end over a reference contract set, not a live deployment. Specifically:

  • The contracts in /contracts are unaudited and not deployed anywhere — FACTORY_ADDRESS in assets/app.js is a placeholder.
  • PriceOracle.sol takes reporter-submitted prices; it doesn't ship a real data pipeline to Zillow, Redfin, county records, or any other source.
  • Nothing here evaluates whether tokenizing a specific physical property makes the resulting coin a security, a real-estate-adjacent financial product, or something requiring registration in your jurisdiction. That's a legal question, not a code path — talk to a lawyer before this touches real money or real property titles.
