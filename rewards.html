Rewards & buyback

Every trade charges a fee, and that fee splits three ways. Holders get 40 percent, the buyback gets 30 percent, and the protocol keeps 30 percent.

Holder rewards are not sent out. Your share builds up in the contract and stays there until you claim it yourself.

The buyback is open to anyone. Any wallet can call it, and the call takes whatever sits in the buyback pot, swaps it for the token, and burns what it buys.

All of this is read straight from the chain. Balances, claimable amounts, and pot size come from the contract itself, with no indexer or database in between.
